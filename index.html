Sometimes, it's the little things you probably don't even think about. But those little things can mean more than you realise. It wasn't because of one particular conversation, one specific thing we did, or because I was trying to turn something between us into more than it was. It happened gradually. The more I got to know you and the more I noticed the person you are, the more I found myself appreciating you in a different way. I started liking you for you. Not for what you could give me, not for what you could do for me, and not because I thought you owed me anything. I liked the person behind all those little moments — your personality, your smile, the way you can brighten someone's day, and the things that make you uniquely you. I don't think you always realise just how special those things are. You deserve to be seen for the person you are. You deserve kindness that doesn't come with expectations, appreciation that isn't conditional, and people who recognise your worth without you having to prove it to them. Somewhere along the way, you became someone I cared about in a way that went beyond an ordinary friendship, and I wanted to acknowledge that honestly. At the same time, I've been reflecting on myself and my own life. Looking back at certain things from my past made me realise that, right now, I don't want to pursue a crush or try to fall in love with anyone. I have things within myself that I need to understand and work through, and I don't want to begin something with someone when I'm not sure I'm ready for it. I would never want you to get caught in the middle of something I'm still figuring out. You haven't done anything wrong, Kaylla, and none of this is your responsibility. I also don't want this message to make you feel like you need to act differently around me. I don't want you to feel awkward, guilty, or as though every interaction between us suddenly has a different meaning. I value the friendship we have, and I don't want to lose the naturalness of it by putting expectations on something that was never promised to me in the first place. You don't owe me feelings just because I developed them for you. You don't owe me a chance because I made you a website. You don't owe me an explanation, a response to this letter, or anything else. If you read this and decide to carry on as normal, that's genuinely okay with me. You can smile at me, joke with me, talk to me, and treat me the same way you always have. You don't have to make this into a big thing. I would rather we continue being ourselves than have you feel like you need to walk on eggshells around me. And please understand something else the kindness behind this website was never fake just because I had feelings for you. I didn't make it only because I had a crush. I made it because I thought you deserved to feel appreciated. The way you make other people feel matters. Your presence matters. And even if you never saw me in the same way I saw you, that doesn't erase the good things I genuinely saw in you. Maybe this is simply one of those moments where I'm learning to let go of expectations, understand myself better, and accept that not every feeling has to become a relationship to have meant something. I'm grateful that I got to know you, that I got to make something for you, and that I got to experience the happiness of doing something kind for someone I care about. I don't regret that.
